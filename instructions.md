# Walkie Talkie Instructions

![Walkie-talkie enclosure and PCB design](assets/walkie-design-overview.png)

1. You must get the lipo battery and solder its wires to the input of the pcb

![Lipo battery connected to the PCB](assets/instructions-01-lipo.png)

2. Then, solder all 5 buttons using the through holes in the pcb

![Five buttons soldered to the PCB](assets/instructions-02-buttons.png)

3. Next, solder on the OLED display through the 4 pin slot

![OLED display connected through the four-pin slot](assets/instructions-03-oled.png)

4. Next, solder on the potentiometer looking at the diagram below

![Potentiometer wiring diagram](assets/instructions-04-potentiometer.png)

5. Print the button pad, and button press stls, and put them as shown in the picture on top of the buttons 

![Button pad and button press parts](assets/instructions-05-button-parts.png)

6. Print the case bottom stl and put everything on there. Use a UFL connector with the SX1262 to connect to the radio antenna

![PCB installed in the case bottom with antenna connection](assets/instructions-06-case-bottom.png)

7. Print the case lid and use super glue to add it on

![Completed case with lid](assets/instructions-07-case-lid.png)
